# Isometric review

Review at actual size (100% zoom) at 24 and 48 px, on white and on near-black.
`emoji-asset-creator`'s review sheet and contact sheet render these cells. Record
accept, revise or uncertain for each check with a short reason; technical
validation does not decide any of them.

## Per asset

1. **Meaning at 24 px:** a person who has not seen the brief names the subject.
2. **Projection:** vertical edges vertical, receding edges at the set's angle, no
   perspective convergence, same 45 degree turn as the anchor.
3. **Three tones:** top, left and right faces are distinguishable at 48 px, and at
   least the top face and one side at 24 px. Light comes from the upper left.
4. **Contrast:** the outer silhouette is visible on both backgrounds; no edge
   disappears into near-black or white.
5. **Palette:** only base hues and one accent, with the accent on the meaningful part.
6. **Simplicity:** 3 to 6 volumes, no texture, no detail smaller than 1/12 of the
   object width, no text, numbers, logos or brand marks.
7. **Clean output:** transparent background with real alpha, no floor, platform,
   contact shadow, halo, checkerboard or cropped edge; content inside the safe area.

## Across the set

- Same elevation and turn; objects do not look like they sit on different floors.
- Comparable visual weight: a pin is not much smaller than a house at 24 px.
- Frontal exceptions (faces, hands) match the set's light, palette and rounding.
- No asset resembles a specific company's illustration or icon closely enough to
  be mistaken for it. When in doubt, mark it uncertain and ask the user.

## Repairs

Change one failed property per request and restate the unchanged style block.
Common repairs: "keep everything, correct the projection to true isometric";
"keep everything, raise the right face lightness so the edge separates on dark";
"keep everything, merge the small details into two larger volumes". Stay inside
the agreed request budget and keep every attempt in the record.
